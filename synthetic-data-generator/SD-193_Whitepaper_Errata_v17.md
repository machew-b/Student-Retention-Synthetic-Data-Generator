# SD-193 Whitepaper v17 — Suggested Errata (from implementing Deliverable 2)

Findings from running the generator at N = 50,000, seed 193. Each item gives the location, what the implementation showed, and suggested replacement wording. These are recommendations for a v18; nothing in the notebook was altered to hide them.

## E-1 · §8 targeting claims are not properties of the Appendix A population (substantive)
**Where:** §8 ("approximately seventy percent of students with genuinely high current vulnerability are correctly identified… approximately twenty-five percent of students who are offered support have genuinely low underlying vulnerability"); Table 4 rows "Intervention targeting precision (true positives) 70 %" and "Intervention false-flag rate 25 %"; §12 edge case 2.

**Finding.** With β₀ = −9, β = 6 on the Appendix A population, only ≈ 6 % of student-terms receive support (Table 7 implies ≈ 5 %). Realized sensitivity is ≈ 16 % (13–25 % across alternative cut-offs for "high current vulnerability"; capped at ≈ 21 % at the illustrative 30 % prevalence). A scan of eight (β₀, β) settings (notebook §7.4) found no setting with sensitivity ≥ 60 % and false-flag share ≤ 40 %; where sensitivity nears 70 %, the false-flag share is ≈ 65 %. The cause is structural: three binary *prior-term* signals cannot see the *current* shock that is part of current vulnerability. "Genuinely high current vulnerability" is also not operationally defined, which makes re-fitting ill-posed.

**Suggested wording (§8, replacing the two quoted sentences):**
> "In the illustrative two-population submodel of Table 3, Eqs. 1–2 flag about 71 % of high-vulnerability student-terms, and about 28 % of flagged student-terms are low-vulnerability. These are properties of that submodel, not calibration targets: in the Appendix A population, where the signals are generated by the Phase 3 forms, the realized figures differ (Appendix A.3), and the two cannot in general be hit jointly because the prior-term signals do not observe the current term's shock."

**Table 4:** change the Basis of both rows to "Design intent of the illustrative submodel; realized values reported as outputs (App. A.3)" and keep the tag ASSUMED. **§12 edge case 2:** replace "consistent with the twenty-five percent false-flag rate in Section 8" with "consistent with a non-zero false-flag share (≈ 22 % under the 30 %-prevalence definition in App. A.3)".

## E-2 · Held-out check verdict is seed-dependent (disclosure)
**Where:** §11 / App. A Table 6. The pilot reports a single FAIL (ratio 1.72). In the notebook the main run gives 1.673 (inside [1.2, 1.7]) but the 20-population distribution is 1.69 ± 0.03 with ≈ 60 % of runs in the band. **Suggested:** state the verdict as the replicate distribution (mean ± SD, share in band) rather than one seed. Band and hazard form are unchanged.

## E-3 · IPW figures in Table 6 could not be reproduced (disclosure)
**Where:** App. A Table 6 ("75.6 % untrimmed; −17.8 % trimmed"). On identical data, four IPW variants (Hajek/Horvitz–Thompson × untrimmed/clipped) give −14 % to +74 %; the pilot figures resemble two of them but the pilot script was not available, so exact agreement could not be checked. The weight maximum (8,104) does reproduce. **Suggested:** report the range across normalisations and say the point is implementation-dependence under near-violated positivity.
